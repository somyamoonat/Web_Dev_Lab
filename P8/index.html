Positioning Mechanics Breakdown

position: sticky

Acts as relative until the user scrolls past its threshold (e.g. top: 0), at which point it sticks to the viewport boundary within its parent container.

position: absolute

Removes the element from normal document flow and positions it exactly relative to its nearest ancestor with position: relative, enabling perfect edge-to-edge overlay curtains.
